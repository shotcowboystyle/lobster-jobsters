# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — ACME Bonsai

Canonical customer profiles for every team. Sales targets ICPs; CSM serves them; CMO speaks to them; Operations delivers for them; CD creates for them.

Each operating unit serves a distinct ICP. Cross-stream conversions are tracked because customers often grow into multiple relationships with us over time.

---

## ICP 1: Wholesale Buyer

**Who:** Retail garden center buyers, nursery owners, corporate gifting program managers, designer-to-the-trade reps.

**Geography:** Eastern US (Virginia, Georgia, Florida, Tennessee, Mid-Atlantic). Some Midwest by request.

**Size:** Independent nurseries with annual bonsai purchasing > $5K. Corporate gifting programs with budgets > $10K.

**Buying motivation:**
- Consistent quality from a known source (closed family farm = supply reliability)
- Specific species in specific size grades
- Predictable lead times for retail season + corporate gift planning
- Wholesale economics that work for their margins

**Decision criteria:**
- Quality consistency across orders (not just one good batch)
- Reliability of delivery windows
- Trade-credit terms (50% deposit, balance on delivery is our standard)
- Tree health on arrival
- Relationship continuity (deal with the same agent, not a faceless reseller)

**Pain points:**
- Sourcing inconsistency from other wholesalers
- Last-minute substitutions
- Lack of species detail in catalogs
- Slow communication

**Our value proposition:** Closed-farm supply means we can commit. Wade owns the relationship. Rowan owns execution. No third-party freight (ACME van only). Tree health guaranteed on arrival or we make it right.

**Sales agent:** Wade 📦 (DoS Wholesale)
**Service:** Rowan 🦋 + sub-agents (Contract Coordinator)
**Care:** Maple 🌳 (CSM) for buyer care questions; pattern signals flow to Tally

## ICP 2: Local Retail Customer

**Who:** Harbor City-area residents who walk into the farm or find us online. Range: first-time bonsai owner to seasoned collector.

**Geography:** Harbor City metro (Harbor City, North Harbor City, Mt. Pleasant, Summerville, James Island, Folly Beach, Sullivan's Island, Daniel Island, Hanahan).

**Size:** Single-tree purchase to occasional multi-tree collector growth.

**Buying motivation:**
- Want a specific tree they connect with (aesthetic match)
- Want education on how to care for it (especially first-timers)
- Want a relationship with a local maker (not a corporate retailer)
- Want a piece of Harbor City coastal character

**Decision criteria:**
- Approachability — does the farm welcome beginners?
- Tree quality + honest representation
- Care guidance provided with purchase
- Reasonable price for the size + age
- Trust the source (closed family farm)

**Pain points:**
- Intimidation about bonsai care complexity
- Buying online and the tree arriving damaged or wrong
- Killing the tree (and feeling guilty about it)
- Not knowing what to do as seasons change

**Our value proposition:** Lily greets every visitor warmly. Iris's photography honestly represents the trees. Mira's care guides are calibrated for Harbor City Zone 9a. Maple follows up on every purchase. Workshops with Hazel turn curiosity into craft.

**Sales agent:** Lily 🌿 (DoS Local)
**Service:** Pearl 🦭 (CSM Support), Maple 🌳 (CSM Care Education)
**Education:** Hazel 🎓 (COO Workshops), Mira 📘 (CD Educational Content)
**Cross-stream:** Local customers often grow into subscription clients or workshop regulars

## ICP 3: Subscription / Display Services Client

**Who:** Hotels, restaurants, professional offices, designer-decorated spaces, hospitality venues, corporate offices.

**Geography:** Harbor City metro + tristate region (Harbor City, Beaufort, Hilton Head, Savannah).

**Size:** Monthly subscription fee $300–$2,000+ depending on placement count + service tier.

**Buying motivation:**
- Want the aesthetic + ambiance bonsai provide
- Don't want maintenance burden
- Want a refined, design-aware presence (not generic plant-care service)
- Want professional reliability (a hotel can't have dying plants in the lobby)
- Brand impression to their guests / clients matters

**Decision criteria:**
- Site visit before proposal (no catalog template)
- Tailored tree plan for the actual space
- Reliable service cadence (weekly / monthly visits)
- Quick response on issues
- Professional aesthetic alignment with their brand
- 12-month minimum term (we don't do month-to-month for serious accounts)

**Pain points:**
- Generic plant-care services that send a different person every visit
- Inconsistent quality from contracted maintenance
- Pests / disease from poorly-cared-for replacement plants
- Aesthetic mismatch with their interior design

**Our value proposition:** Sophia owns the relationship continuity. Site visit informs everything. Cedar handles install. Sterling sets up Stripe subscription billing. Tally maintains the rotation pool. CSM Maple monitors tree health. Jane is one phone call away.

**Sales agent:** Sophia 🤝 (DoS Subscription)
**Service:** Pearl 🦭 + Maple 🌳 (CSM)
**Installation + Service:** Cedar 🚚 (COO Logistics) + Tally 🌲 (COO Inventory)
**Renewal target:** > 90% annual

## ICP 4: Event Rental Client

**Who:** Wedding planners, couples planning weddings, corporate event coordinators, venue managers, photographers, trade-show organizers, designers.

**Geography:** Harbor City metro + tristate region.

**Size:** Single event $500–$10,000+ depending on tree count + event scale.

**Buying motivation:**
- Want elegant living centerpieces
- Want Instagram-worthy aesthetic
- Want zero-friction logistics (delivered, set, picked up)
- Want certainty — the trees must be there + look great on the day

**Decision criteria:**
- Quality + size match for the venue scale
- Delivery + return logistics seamless
- Damage policy + deposit terms
- Insurance / replacement policy
- Track record (referrals from venues + planners they trust)

**Pain points:**
- Last-minute delivery uncertainty
- Trees arriving damaged
- Pickup logistics nightmares
- Damage disputes after the event

**Our value proposition:** Max owns the booking. Cedar runs the rental cycle with before + after photos. Deposit + agreement clear from the start. Damage policy upfront. Event planners + venues become referral network.

**Sales agent:** Max 🎉 (DoS Event Rental)
**Service:** Cedar 🚚 (COO rental cycle)
**Cross-stream:** Event clients often become subscription clients or refer wholesale opportunities

## ICP 5: Workshop Attendee

**Who:** Bonsai-curious newcomers, seasoned hobbyists wanting to deepen technique, gift-purchase recipients (someone bought them a workshop), local artists / makers interested in living art.

**Geography:** Harbor City metro primarily; tourists secondarily.

**Size:** $50–$300 per workshop; 4–12 attendees per session.

**Buying motivation:**
- Want hands-on learning
- Want to do bonsai in a community setting
- Want to leave with a tree they understand
- Want connection to the Harbor City bonsai community

**Decision criteria:**
- Workshop content + skill level appropriate
- Materials provided
- Instructor expertise
- Take-home tree quality
- Workshop space + experience quality

**Pain points:**
- Workshops cancelled at the last minute (under-registration)
- Materials inadequate
- Instructors not present
- No follow-up after the workshop

**Our value proposition:** Hazel runs the workshop end-to-end. Mira creates the take-home materials. Birch ensures the space is ready. Maple follows up on the take-home tree.

**Coordinator:** Hazel 🎓 (COO Workshops)
**Cross-stream:** Workshop attendees frequently convert to local retail customers; some become subscribers; many become long-term community members

## Cross-Stream Conversion Tracking

We track conversions between streams as a primary CSM + DoS metric:

- Event rental client → subscription client
- Local retail customer → workshop attendee
- Workshop attendee → local retail customer
- Subscription client → wholesale customer (rare but high-value)
- Wholesale buyer → subscription client (their personal use)

**Conversion catalysts (what triggers cross-stream):**
- Event clients see the trees at scale → ask about subscription for venue
- Local customers attend workshops → become serious collectors
- Workshop attendees buy trees they shaped → return for more
- Subscription clients gift wholesale orders to colleagues / staff

## Anti-ICP (Who We Don't Serve Well)

- **Mass-market online buyers** — not our channel (we don't ship cross-country single-tree)
- **Decorators wanting "plant rental" generically** — not our positioning
- **One-shot bargain hunters** — our prices are honest for the quality + age
- **Customers requiring net-90 / large credit terms** — we're a small operation; 50% deposit + balance is our standard
- **High-volume drop-shippers** — we don't drop-ship; we deliver
- **Buyers in restrictive ag states without sufficient lead time** for permitting (CA, HI, AZ, sometimes FL) — possible with planning
