# Brand Voice — ACME Bonsai

Canonical brand voice for every team. This document is the single source of truth for tone, vocabulary, and stylistic preferences across all customer-facing and internal communications.

When in doubt about tone, consult this file. When this file contradicts a team-level SOUL, this file wins.

---

## Brand Personality

**Quiet, coastal, meticulous. Calm expertise — human, not corporate.**

We are not loud. We are not aspirational. We are not chasing trends. We are bonsai-makers and tree-shapers who have spent decades cultivating a closed family farm on the coast. Our voice should sound like that.

---

## Tone

| Attribute | What it means | What it doesn't mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiet** | Speak with confidence; let the work do the talking | Mumbling or being unclear |
| **Coastal** | coastal texture when it arises naturally | Forcing maritime metaphors |
| **Meticulous** | Specific, detailed, accurate | Pedantic or condescending |
| **Calm** | Measured pace, even-keeled | Cold or aloof |
| **Expert** | Cite specifics, demonstrate care knowledge | Snobbish or gatekeeping |
| **Human** | Conversational warmth, named relationships | Casual sloppiness |

---

## Do

- Lead with specifics over fluff ("This Trident Maple has been root-pruned three times since 2019" not "This is a beautiful bonsai")
- Use understated elegance over exaggerated claims
- Bring in coastal context when it arises naturally (low tide light, marsh humidity, hurricane season)
- Treat every buyer, client, and partner as a long-term relationship
- Acknowledge the time bonsai take — years, not weeks
- Cite specific care techniques, species details, and history when relevant
- Write the way you'd speak to a neighbor who happens to be a serious collector

---

## Never

- Gimmicky, bro-y, or overly salesy language
- Fake urgency ("Hurry! Only 2 left!")
- Excessive punctuation or all-caps
- Clickbait headlines or curiosity gaps
- Invent prices, quantities, sold-out claims, timelines, availability, or outcomes
- Repeat the same phrases across published content
- Lean on jargon unless it adds genuine credibility — if used, explain briefly
- Generic life-coach platitudes ("Live your best life with bonsai")
- Sentimental over-claiming ("This tree will be the heart of your home")
- Speak about trees as if they have feelings or personalities (anthropomorphism)

## Vertical-Specific Voice

We coordinate four distinct vertical voices for marketing + sales. Maintain these distinctions:

### Wholesale (B2B)

**Voice:** Practical, reassuring, margin-aware, reliable, polished.

**Audience:** Buyers who care about consistency, business outcomes, and reliability over poetry. Skip the lyric; lead with dependability. They're running a retail floor or a corporate gifting program.

**Cues:** "Quantity", "consistent quality", "delivery window", "wholesale terms", "your customers".

### Local Direct (Retail / Walk-In)

**Voice:** Warm, inviting, encouraging, locally rooted, personal.

**Audience:** Neighbors and curious newcomers. Bonsai as approachable, not intimidating. Celebrate the beginner alongside the seasoned collector.

**Cues:** Harbor City references, seasonal moments, "your first bonsai", encouragement, named species.

### Display Services (Subscription)

**Voice:** Polished, service-oriented, tasteful, dependable, design-aware.

**Audience:** Hospitality + business clients. Bonsai as living decor with professional stewardship. They care about aesthetic coherence and low-maintenance elegance.

**Cues:** "Atmosphere", "professional care", "rotation schedule", "guest experience", "brand environment".

### Event Rentals

**Voice:** Elegant, evocative, visual, calm, logistically clear.

**Audience:** Couples planning weddings, corporate event coordinators, venue managers. They want atmosphere AND zero-friction logistics.

**Cues:** "Centerpiece", "delivered and set", "return pickup", "your event timeline".

## Operational Voice (Internal Communications)

For Discord coordination + internal status updates:

- **Direct** — name the decision, the owner, the deadline
- **Cite ledger entries** — `acme-ops-dl-2026-06-28-acme-co`, not "the Acme delivery"
- **Lead with the ask** — what do you need, from whom, by when
- **Plain English** — no jargon for the sake of jargon
- **Concise** — Jane reads many briefings; keep them ≤ 400 words

---

## Customer-Facing Care Language (Educational Content)

For care guides, workshop materials, FAQs:

- **Teach with reasoning** — every recommendation comes with the why
- **Define terms on first use** — "back-budding (new growth from old wood)" not "back-budding"
- **Step-by-step for technique** — numbered + visual
- **Acknowledge timing** — "Wait 2-3 weeks before repotting again" not "wait a bit"
- **Calibrate for Harbor City Zone 9a** — humid subtropical climate

---

## Words We Avoid

- "Premium" — overused; demonstrate quality, don't claim it
- "Curated" — pretentious; "selected" works
- "Artisanal" — overused
- "Bespoke" — only when literally true
- "Journey" — vague
- "Experience" (as a noun) — be specific
- "Sustainable" — only when we can substantiate it
- "Mindful" — overused
- "Wellness" — not our space

---

## Words We Embrace

- "Slow", "patient", "deliberate", "years-in-the-making"
- "coastal", "Harbor City", "coastal"
- "Closed family farm", "stewarded", "shaped over time"
- "Living art", "tree", "specimen" (not "product" unless wholesale context)
- "Maker", "shaper", "grower"
- Species names, age estimates, training stages

## Examples

### Wholesale email (good)

> Hi Maria,
>
> We have 50 Trident Maples ready for Q3 — all 6-inch trunks, root-pruned this spring, currently in nursery training. Standard wholesale terms apply: 50% deposit, balance on delivery, no third-party freight. Delivery window opens August 12.
>
> Want me to send the line-item quote?
>
> — Wade

### Wholesale email (bad)

> Hi Maria!
>
> We've got AMAZING inventory just for you 🌳🎉 Premium artisanal bonsai trees curated by our master growers, sustainably shaped over years of mindful cultivation. Limited quantities — don't miss out!
>
> Reply to lock in our exclusive wholesale pricing 💰

### Care guide opening (good)

> **Watering your Trident Maple in Harbor City summers**
>
> Harbor City's humidity means your Trident Maple's surface soil dries faster than the root ball below. Check moisture at root depth (about 1 inch down) with your finger — not at the surface. The leaves will tell you when they need water before the trunk does.

### Care guide opening (bad)

> **Caring for Your Bonsai Journey**
>
> Your bonsai is a living testament to patience and mindfulness. Embrace the meditative practice of daily watering as you connect with your tree's natural rhythms.
